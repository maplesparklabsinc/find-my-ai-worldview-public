About Stewards

You expect AI to bring real benefits and real risks, and you think the answer is enforceable rules: independent testing, legal responsibility, regulators with the power to act. You see oversight less as a brake than as the condition for trusting the technology, and you would rather get the institutions right than slow the technology down.

The strongest objection to this view

Regulators may lack the expertise and speed to oversee a fast-moving field, and detailed rules can entrench the largest companies.
