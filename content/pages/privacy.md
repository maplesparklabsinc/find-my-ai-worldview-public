### Privacy

*Last updated: {privacy_last_updated}*

We built this to need as little about you as possible. Here's exactly what happens.

**When you take the quiz**
We save an anonymous copy of your answers, how long each question took, your guess about where your answers landed (if you made one), your result, the date you finished (not the time), and which version of the quiz you took. We don't save your name, your email, your IP address, or anything about your device, and we don't use cookies to track you. Your result gets a random link so you can come back to it and share it. Anyone you give that link to can see your result, but nothing on it says who you are. Your guess isn't on that page or in anything you share; only the browser you took the quiz in shows it to you.

We use these anonymous answers to count how many people have taken the quiz, see how people answer overall, and improve the questions and the perspectives.

**If you leave your email**
Only if you choose to. We keep your email in a separate, locked-down place, linked to your anonymous answers so we can invite a mix of people to follow-ups. We use it only to contact you about this project. We never sell it, share it or add it to a mailing list. To be removed, email {contact_email} and we'll delete it.

**If you send us feedback**
We keep what you write. If you add an email, we use it only to reply. If you tick the box, we may quote you without your name on our What's changed page.

**Visitor statistics**
We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits. It doesn't use cookies or identify you.

**Where your data lives**
Answers, emails and feedback are stored with Supabase in Canada. The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel briefly processes your IP address to deliver the site; we don't store it.

**What we'll never do**
Sell data. Use advertising trackers. Try to work out who you are from your answers. Use anyone's results to make decisions about them, like hiring, lending, admissions or political targeting.

**How long we keep things**
Anonymous answers: as long as the project runs. Emails: until you ask us to remove them, or until the project ends. Feedback: as long as the project runs.

**Age**
The quiz is meant for adults. Only people 18 or older can leave an email.
