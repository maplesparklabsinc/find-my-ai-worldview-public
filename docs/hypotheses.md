# What we expect to find

We wrote these down before many people had taken the quiz, so we can't quietly fit the story to the data afterwards. Each one says what we'd look at and what would count against it.

Status: **Not yet looked at** → **Early signs** (under about 500 responses, a self-selected group) → **Tested** (enough responses, from a broad enough mix of people, to take seriously). There's no "proven". Observations are added with dates, and never deleted, including when they go against us.

All early results come from people who chose to take the quiz, starting with friends of the founder. They describe those people, not the public.

## H1. Expecting good and expecting harm aren't opposites

**Claim:** many people expect AI to do a lot of good *and* a lot of harm.
**How we'll check:** the share of people in the upper half of both "How much good" and "How much harm".
**Would count against it:** most people high on one are low on the other.
**Status:** Not yet looked at.
**Observations:**
- (none yet)

## H2. People back public rules in principle but aren't sure where the line is

**Claim:** support for public rules is broad, but people hesitate when the question gets specific.
**How we'll check:** "Who sets the rules" leans to public rules overall, while answers to the more specific statements on that theme are more mixed, people are less settled on that theme, and feedback says so.
**Would count against it:** the specific statements get the same strong answers as the general ones.
**Status:** Not yet looked at.
**Observations:**
- 2026-10-16: the founder's own test run. He wanted rules with real consequences but wasn't sure how strict they should be. One person, noted as where the idea came from, not as evidence.

## H3. Perspective labels hide real agreement

**Claim:** people with different results often agree on most themes and differ on one or two.
**How we'll check:** for pairs of perspectives, the themes where most of both groups land on the same side.
**Would count against it:** people with different perspectives differ across most themes.
**Status:** Not yet looked at.
**Observations:**
- (none yet)

## H4. Frequent AI users expect more good, and also worry more about specific harms

**Claim:** using AI often goes with more expected good *and* more concern about particular harms, not less concern.
**How we'll check:** "How much good" and "How much harm" by how often people say they use AI tools (D1).
**Would count against it:** frequent users expect more good and less harm.
**Status:** Not yet looked at. Needs enough people in each usage group.
**Observations:**
- (none yet)

## H5. Some statement pairs aren't true opposites

**Claim:** for some pairs of statements meant to point in opposite directions, many people agree with both.
**How we'll check:** the "agree with both" flag in the question health table on the admin dashboard.
**Would count against it:** few people agree with both statements in any pair.
**Status:** Not yet looked at. An outside review found this before launch, and those statements were rewritten.
**Observations:**
- (none yet)
